Privacy Policy

Swipe Quest · Last updated: 29 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swipe Quest ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. By using Swipe Quest, you agree to the practices described here.

Plain-language summary: Swipe Quest is a single-player puzzle game. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts. Your game progress is stored on your own device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect certain device and usage information to serve those ads. Details are below.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

Swipe Quest does not require you to register or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contact list.

Information stored on your device

Your game data — such as levels completed, stars earned, coins, hearts, cosmetic choices, and settings — is saved locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Information collected automatically by advertising

Swipe Quest displays advertisements using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process certain information, which can include:

  • Device identifiers (such as an advertising identifier / IDFA, where permitted)
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • General location inferred from your IP address (not precise GPS location)
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped

This information is collected directly by Google under its own privacy policy. We do not control and do not receive this raw data ourselves. You can review how Google uses this information here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2. How We Use Information

Because game data stays on your device, we use it only to run the game for you — to remember your progress, hearts, coins, and preferences. Advertising information is used by Google to display relevant ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance.

3. Advertising and Your Choices

Swipe Quest uses ads to keep the game free. Some ads are rewarded (you choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward such as a hint or a heart); others may appear between levels.

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization.

On first launch in some regions, you may be shown a consent request (via Google's consent tools) asking whether Google may use your data for personalized ads. Your choice there controls how ads are personalized.

4. Children's Privacy

Swipe Quest is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us and we will address it. If you are a parent or guardian, you can use the device-level ad and privacy controls described above.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties except as needed for the advertising services described above, where Google acts as an independent provider under its own policies.

6. Data Retention

Game data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Information processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, most such requests relating to advertising should be directed to Google. You can also exercise choices directly through the device settings listed in Section 3. For any request to us, use the contact details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Since your game data stays on your device, its security also depends on your device's own protections.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
